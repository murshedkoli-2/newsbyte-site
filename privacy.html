Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy covers the NewsByte website and NewsByte Publisher, the in-house tool NewsByte uses to upload its own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, Facebook and TikTok.

Who uses NewsByte Publisher

NewsByte Publisher is used only by the NewsByte team, to publish videos that NewsByte made to accounts that NewsByte owns. It is not offered to the public and does not let anyone sign in to it.

What the tool accesses

When the NewsByte team connects its own accounts, the tool receives access tokens from those platforms with only the permissions needed to publish:

  • YouTube: uploading videos, thumbnails and captions to the NewsByte channel.
  • Facebook: posting videos and comments on the NewsByte Page.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name) and uploading videos to the NewsByte account's inbox, where they are posted by hand.

The tool does not read, collect or store viewers' data, followers' data, messages, or any information about other people.

Where data is kept

Access tokens are stored only on the NewsByte team's own computer, in a local configuration file. They are not sent to any server other than the platform they belong to, are never shared or sold, and are not used for advertising.

This website

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from each platform's settings (Google Account → Security → Third-party access; Facebook → Settings → Business integrations; TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Removing access deletes nothing on the platform but stops the tool from publishing.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent to us through our Facebook Page.
